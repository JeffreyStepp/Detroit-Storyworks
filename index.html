About Detroit Storyworks

Detroit is bigger than one landmark or one era.

Detroit Storyworks connects visitors with different parts of the city through stories, places, objects, art, music, engineering, and shared experiences. Our goal is to make local history feel active, current, and connected to the Detroit people know today.

Why stories matter

The city is still being written.

History is not only something behind glass. Detroit's music, manufacturing, neighborhoods, art, food, sports, and riverfront continue to change. Our experiences connect the past with what visitors can see and hear around them now.
